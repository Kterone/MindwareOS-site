01 WHAT IS MINDWAREOS

  

    

      
WHAT

      
MindwareOS is a custom, Arch Linux based distribution delivered as one installer script that you run from the Arch live environment.

    

    

      
WHY

      
Arch gives full control but its manual setup is hard for newcomers. Mainstream systems bundle software, accounts and telemetry you never chose.

    

    

      
WHAT IT SOLVES

      
It removes the pain of installing Arch by hand, keeps Arch's minimalism, and stays transparent: every step is plain bash you can read.

    

  






  
02 HOW IT WORKS

  
The installer runs as a sequence of stages. Each one is announced on screen and logged to /tmp/mindware-install.log.

  
    
    
  1. PRE-FLIGHTDetects UEFI or BIOS, checks internet and RAM, syncs the clock, refreshes mirrors.
    2. 
    
  2. DISKLists disks, warns before wiping, asks for ext4 or BTRFS and a swap size.
    3. 
    
  3. IDENTITYUsername, hostname, timezone and passwords.
    4. 
    
  4. DESKTOPKDE Plasma, GNOME, XFCE, i3, Hyprland, MATE, Cinnamon or none.
    5. 
    
  5. HARDWAREFinds NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics, VM guests and CPU microcode.
    6. 
    
  6. OPTIONALAudio, Bluetooth, AUR helper and printing, all opt-in.
    7. 
    
  7. PARTITIONGPT or MBR layout, formatting, BTRFS subvolumes, swap and mounts.
    8. 
    
  8. BASE + CHROOTpacstrap, fstab, locale, users, branding, desktop, drivers and GRUB.

04 HOW TO USE

  
⚠ WARNING: the installer wipes the whole disk you select. Back up first, and try it in a virtual machine before real hardware.


  

    
1

      
Boot the Arch live ISO

      
Download the official Arch Linux ISO from archlinux.org, write it to a USB drive (or attach it to a VM) and boot it. Connect to the internet. Wired works out of the box; for Wi-Fi use iwctl.

    


    
2

      
Get the script

      
Download install.sh with the button above, or fetch it in the live shell:

      
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kterone/MindwareOS/main/install.sh

    


    
3

      
Read it first

      
It is plain bash. Skim it so you know exactly what it will do to your disk.

      
less install.sh

    


    
4

      
Run it as root

      
The live ISO already gives you a root shell.

      
bash install.sh

    


    
5

      
Answer the prompts

      
Pick the target disk (for example sda or nvme0n1), confirm the wipe, choose ext4 or BTRFS, set swap, username, hostname, timezone and passwords, choose a desktop and any optional extras.

    


    
6

      
Reboot and log in

      
When the installer finishes, remove the USB and reboot. Then open a terminal and try:

      
mind help

    

  


  
Tip: avoid piping unverified scripts straight into a shell (curl ... | bash). Download, read, then run, as above.






  
05 PRIVACY

  
Based on a review of the published installer source (the final ~150 lines could not be reviewed).

  

    
TELEMETRY
The reviewed installer sets up no analytics, crash-reporting or usage-tracking service, and no vendor account.

    
DATA COLLECTION
Nothing is sent anywhere. Your usernames and passwords stay on your machine; the install log lives in /tmp.

    
NETWORK CONTACTS
archlinux.org (ping), NTP servers, Arch mirrors, and the AUR only if you opt in.

    
COOKIES
The OS sets none. Browser cookies and telemetry follow Firefox defaults, since no hardened profile is applied yet.

  

  

    
KNOWN LIMITS (v1.0.0)

    
    
      
  • No disk encryption (LUKS) offered in the reviewed part.
    • 
      
  • Passwords are handled in plaintext during install.
    • 
      
  • No firewall, DNS-over-TLS or MAC randomisation configured yet.
